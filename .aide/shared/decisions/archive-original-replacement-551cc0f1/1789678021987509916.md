# archive-original-replacement

**Decision:** Explicit replace-original for sessions and schedules replaces the original group, retires its alongside copy and retargets the same source mapping in one backup-guarded atomic transaction. Before retiring a copy, compare its entire destination group with saved import fingerprints, including messages absent from the incoming archive and additions without a baseline. Destination edits or deletions are explicit blocking conflicts, not acknowledged by the replacement flag or a fresh backup. Serialize all overwritten or deleted destination IDs in the content-free removed preview. Interactive selections include replace-original for mapped supported groups and supersede every prior choice; blocked retirements must be reconciled, replaced in place or skipped. Unchanged retries remain no-ops.

## Rationale

PR #321 removed records before checking destination fingerprints and hid deletion inventory. Backup state guards protect changes after backup, not edits already captured in it. Preserve the stable-import contract while making the distinct destructive operation visible and testable.
