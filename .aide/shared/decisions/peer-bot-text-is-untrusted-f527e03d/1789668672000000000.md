# peer-bot-text-is-untrusted

**Decision:** Text written by another bot is wrapped as untrusted before it reaches a turn

## Rationale

Inter-bot messages carry the same risk as a fetched page: a bot that has read hostile content can relay it. inboxPrompt and ask_bot interpolated sender, subject and body straight into the turn, which combined with inbox_post being seeded default-allow let injected instructions cross a bot boundary and arrive looking like the operator's brief. Both now go through promptgen.WrapContext, whose NeutraliseDelimiters stops a payload closing the tag and writing its own. The framing lead stays outside the wrapper because the lead is ours.

## Details

The seeding half of that argument has since been addressed on its own terms: bot_create, bot_update and bot_delete no longer ship a default-allow rule, on the same reasoning that put the soul_* family in noSeedTools. inbox_post, ask_bot and tell_bot remain seeded, because may_message is an explicit declared graph validated as a DAG on write and enforced by building the child's registry without ask_bot — the reach of those three is bounded by structure, not by the seed.
