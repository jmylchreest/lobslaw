# bot-teams-ownership

**Decision:** A bot belongs to exactly one team; authority over a bot comes from that team's owner

## Rationale

Teams exist because 'who answers when I message on Telegram' needs an answer per team rather than one globally, and each team owns its coordinator. One team per bot because a bot with two managers has two sets of instructions about what matters. Authority is derived from the team rather than stored on the bot so the two cannot disagree: the first cut gave teams owners and left bots ungoverned, which made the team check bypassable one route over — a bot you could not move out of someone's team you could still re-brief or delete, including the coordinator that decides who answers for it. An empty group_id reads as the default team, so upgrades need no migration.

## Details

Ownership lives on GroupRecord.owner. groupMayModify answers 'may this principal change this team', and mayModifyBot answers the bot question by looking up the bot's team and asking the same function — there is no second rule to keep in step. A group lookup that fails is a refusal, not a pass: a bot whose team has been deleted is not thereby open to everyone. Writes are gated on the DESTINATION as well as the source, so a move cannot be used to place a bot in a team you do not own.
