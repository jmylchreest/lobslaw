# snapshot-restore-publication

**Decision:** Stage, validate and sync physical snapshots before publishing the replacement Store handle. Serialize restore and close; preserve a durable previous-file link until installation succeeds. A failed rollback disables the store and stops Raft and the node; startup refuses unresolved recovery markers. Cleanup errors after publication are warnings so FSM and Raft acknowledge the committed restore.

## Rationale

Issue 296: recoverable I/O failures must not strand all Store users on a closed handle, and ambiguous disk state must not silently restart.
