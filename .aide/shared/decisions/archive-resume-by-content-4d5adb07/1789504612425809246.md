# archive-resume-by-content

**Decision:** Portable imports resume by re-supplying the same authenticated archive and explicit owner options. Deterministic content/options IDs identify encrypted per-batch source receipts committed atomically with destination records through Raft. Completed receipts prevent replay from overwriting later edits or deletions.

## Rationale

Avoid persisting an additional potentially 256 MiB upload job in every node while retaining durable resumability across restarts and leader changes. The operator already retains the immutable encrypted source archive.
